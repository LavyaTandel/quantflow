## Methodology

- One Node.js process drives the full mix through `RedisBookStore` (`applyDepthDiff`/`topN`/`getMeta`) over a single connection, plus the raw node-redis client for PING/INFO control measurements.
- Issued mix held at ~70/25/5 by scheduling whichever op type is furthest behind its target share.
- Depth diffs carry strictly increasing update IDs per symbol and are serialized per symbol, so continuity stays clean; any non-`applied` result counts as an anomaly and fails that leg's verdict.
- **Redis-side measurement method**: `INFO commandstats` deltas before/after each leg give per-command call counts and cumulative service µs — a **mean**, since Redis does not expose server-side percentiles. Percentile targets are therefore evaluated on client-observed round-trip samples (upper bound: includes localhost transit), alongside the commandstats mean as the closest available server-side statistic.
- Percentile definition: nearest-rank on the ascending-sorted sample — index = ceil(p/100 × n) − 1, clamped to [0, n−1]; p50/p99/max computed over all recorded samples of the phase unless stated otherwise.
